# Optimus Combat Strategy & Tactical Notes
# Chunking note: RECURSIVE / STRUCTURE-AWARE chunks. These are procedures with ordered
# steps - a generic fixed-size splitter would risk cutting a step mid-sentence and
# silently dropping a threshold value, exactly like the course's ingestion mistake story.

Procedure: Insecticon Swarm Engagement (validated during the Darkmount incident)
Step 1: Divert non-essential power (holographic projectors, base climate control) to primary weapon systems.
Step 2: Prioritize target selection by proximity to organic lifeforms first, then by Insecticon weapon class.
Step 3: If swarm count exceeds 12 simultaneous contacts, request vanguard backup rather than engaging solo.
Step 4: Maintain a minimum 15% Energon reserve at all times for emergency thruster burst.
Step 5: If Energon Core output drops below 20% mid-engagement, disengage and return to The Ark - do not
attempt to finish the fight on reserve power.

Procedure: Decepticon Warlord Engagement (validated against the Megatron incident debrief)
Step 1: Establish target's primary weapon signature and attack pattern before closing distance.
Step 2: Keep a minimum 3-second thruster reserve for evasive maneuvering at all times.
Step 3: If target demonstrates an unknown dark Energon capability, retreat to observation range and
reassess rather than continuing to press the engagement blind.
Step 4: Loop in any nearby allied units (Teletraan-monitored assets) before escalating to a
high-risk maneuver.

Procedure: Emergency Extraction (Commander incapacitated or chassis critically damaged)
Step 1: Immediately switch to autopilot return-to-base, overriding manual control.
Step 2: Broadcast a distress signal on the private Autobot frequency and to any allied unit in range.
Step 3: If altitude is above 500 meters and thruster failure is detected, deploy the emergency
stasis pod system before attempting any other recovery action.
