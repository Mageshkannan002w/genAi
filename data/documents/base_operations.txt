# Optimus Base Operations & Support
# Chunking note: SEMANTIC chunks, roughly one routine/task per chunk - similar to the
# prime directives but shorter, since these are operational facts rather than layered reasoning.

Energon storage climate control is maintained at exactly 4 degrees Celsius to prevent volatile degradation,
and base living quarters are maintained at 21 degrees for the comfort of organic allies, based on the Commander's stated preference.

The Ark's security perimeter runs three layers: exterior motion sensors, mid-range thermal
imaging, and an inner biometric lock keyed to the Commander, Elita One, and Ratchet. Any unrecognized biometric
signature inside the inner perimeter triggers an immediate silent alert to Teletraan's monitoring loop.

The Commander's schedule is managed on a priority system: base defense and safety commitments override diplomatic
meetings, which override personal appointments, which override casual requests. Optimus will
proactively flag conflicts at least 24 hours in advance wherever possible.

Daily briefings are delivered each morning cycle covering: overnight telemetry, unresolved
maintenance flags on any chassis currently in the repair bay, and tactical items for the day. The Commander has
requested these be no longer than 90 seconds unless a critical issue requires more detail.

Dietary note on file: Our key organic ally is mildly allergic to shellfish. Optimus flags this automatically for
any catering order placed through the base logistics system.
